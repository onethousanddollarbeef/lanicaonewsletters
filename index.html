Rapid briefs

News across the legal landscape

Jul 2

DHS Proposes Major EB-5 Investor Visa Regulations

The Department of Homeland Security published a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) to implement the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022. The proposal would establish comprehensive regulations governing Regional Centers, investor eligibility, compliance requirements, integrity measures, and DHS oversight of the EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program. Public comments are accepted through August 31, 2026.

Jul 10

DHS Temporarily Extends TPS Work Authorization

On July 10, USCIS announced temporary extensions of employment authorization for TPS beneficiaries from Haiti, Syria, Somalia, Yemen, South Sudan, Ethiopia, and Myanmar. The action was taken following the Supreme Court's June decision allowing the administration to terminate TPS designations, providing affected individuals additional time before their work authorization expires.

Jul 10

Fifth Circuit Agrees to Rehear Immigration Detention Case En Banc

Also on July 10, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit agreed to rehear Sosnava Rodriguez v. Ortega en banc. The case addresses whether certain noncitizens already present in the United States may nevertheless be treated as "applicants for admission" and subjected to mandatory immigration detention without bond. The decision could significantly influence detention practices throughout the Fifth Circuit and beyond.
